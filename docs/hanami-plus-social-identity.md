# Hanami+ Social Identity

This V2 segment extends existing Hanami social systems without replacing their permissions or visibility rules.

## Hanami+ creative editing

Hanami+ controls are character-specific and include display-name presentation, status presentation, decorative profile badges, friend-group organization, social post card styling, reaction/sticker presentation, and guestbook presentation.

Saved creative settings remain attached to the character after Hanami+ expires. An active Hanami+ entitlement is required to edit these creative settings again.

## Standard Hanami social access

Friends, Top Friends, posts, comments, reactions, guestbooks, message permissions, profile visibility, post visibility, and school/staff roles remain standard Hanami systems. Decorative badges do not grant or impersonate staff permissions.

## Privacy

The Social Identity tables are layered on top of the existing published-profile and social-post visibility policies. Styling does not broaden who may read a profile or post.
